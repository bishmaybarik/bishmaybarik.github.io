# About

Hey, I'm Bishmay. I am a Research Associate at the [Centre for Advanced Financial Research and Learning (CAFRAL)](https://www.cafral.org.in/), Reserve Bank of India. I hold a Master's degree in Economics from Shiv Nadar University (Dean's List, 2025) and a Bachelor's in Economics from XIM University, Bhubaneswar.

These days I'm mostly interested in granular development economics questions around caste and migration in India. I like using CS and DS techniques to answer these economics questions, and I'd like my research to be useful enough to actually feed into policy. Previously, I've been a Research Assistant at [QuantEcon](https://quantecon.org/) and a Data Science Intern at [Development Data Lab](https://www.devdatalab.org/).

I'm open to opportunities and happy to collaborate. Feel free to reach out at [bishmaykbarik@gmail.com](mailto:bishmaykbarik@gmail.com).

### Software and Tools

Python, Stata, LaTeX, Git/GitHub, Bash, GNU Make, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, scikit-learn, OpenCV, DuckDB, Parquet, Plotly/Dash, Streamlit

Lately I've been building LLM and agentic pipelines for economics research: fine-tuned MuRIL/BERT classifiers for Hindi and Hinglish text, vision-model extraction from scanned archival tables, and PII-safe workflows using the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs.
